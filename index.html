Higgs Video Uploader

Higgs Video Uploader is a private command-line tool used by a single independent developer (higgs1729) to upload videos to, and manage, the developer's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

The tool uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) to:

  • upload videos that the developer has produced, together with their title, description, tags, thumbnail, scheduled publish time, audience setting (made for kids or not) and altered or synthetic content disclosure;
  • update the metadata and privacy status of the developer's own videos;
  • read the performance statistics of the developer's own channels.

Every upload is started by the channel owner, and only to channels the owner has authorized through Google OAuth.
